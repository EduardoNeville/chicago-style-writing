# The judgment half of verification

The linter checks the mechanical half and cites a rule number or `house`. This file
checks what a regex cannot: whether the text says something, whether it still sounds
like its writer, and whether the artifact is honest about where the words came from.

Answer each question pass or fail. If any answer is fail, fix the draft and run both
halves again. Do not answer "not applicable" for a question that applies to the
register you declared.

## Say something

1. Does the text contain any pattern from `references/15-rhetoric.md`, including the
   five the linter cannot check: fake-profound kicker, synonym cycling, robotic
   rhythm, interpretive metadiscourse, formatting slop?
2. Does every generic sentence pass the portability test, or was it cut or made
   specific to this subject?
3. Is every specific fact preserved? No number smoothed into a claim, no mechanism
   replaced by an abstraction.
4. Does every attribution name a source, or was the claim cut? (Pass 5)
5. Is the ending a concrete point, a takeaway, or a next action? No recap, no
   metaphor standing in for a conclusion.

## Sound like the writer

6. Does the text still sound like its writer: vocabulary, cadence, bluntness, humor,
   uncertainty, digressions?
7. Was anything rewritten for consistency that was already strong?
8. Is the amount of cutting proportional to the actual slop, or was the piece
   compressed until the character left it?

## Match the register

9. Is the register declared and obeyed? `spoken` spells out its numerals, `display`
   carries no terminal punctuation and no sentence structure, `ui` uses label case.
10. Do headings survive being lifted out of the page, and are they parallel across a
    level? (1.61)
11. Do numerals, abbreviations, hyphenation, and dashes follow the style sheet rather
    than the editor's taste?

## Be honest about the source

12. Were all linter errors fixed, or is each exception justified in writing with a
    rule number or `house` and a reason? (quoted matter, code, deliberate nonstandard
    form in dialogue)
13. Is every quoted passage unchanged except its wrapper: quotation marks, dash
    length, initial capitalization, terminal punctuation, note reference, typography?
    (12.67, 12.7)
14. If the words live in another artifact: is the inventory the source of truth, was
    it linted in its own register, and is the frozen hash still current?

## Report it

15. Does the reply contain the full text plus the report, with each change citing its
    rule number or `house`?
16. Is the report proportional: the four-part shape for a document, three lines for a
    paragraph or a button label?
17. For an audit request: findings only, each pattern named with the line quoted and
    a short fix, with no rewrite, no score, and no claim about who or what wrote it?
