Suppose the report was commissioned in March. It found that the finance team had
misallocated costs across the 2019–2022 budget cycle. The reserve fund, which the
board approved in a three-hour meeting attended by all eleven committee members,
was not adequately documented; no receipts were retained. The auditors found three
separate approval stages where the process required one. In a memo dated March 14,
